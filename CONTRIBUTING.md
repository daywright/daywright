# Contributing

Thanks for helping build Daywright. Everyone who contributes code, hardware or
docs is one of its wrights. Our [code of conduct](https://github.com/daywright/.github/blob/main/CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md)
applies everywhere the project lives.

## Sign off your commits

We use the [Developer Certificate of Origin](https://developercertificate.org)
instead of a contributor license agreement. Add a sign-off to every commit with
`git commit -s`, which appends:

```
Signed-off-by: Your Name <you@example.com>
```

By signing off you certify the DCO for that contribution.

## The license of your contribution

Inbound equals outbound: a change is licensed under the license of the folder it
touches (see `LICENSE.md` and `REUSE.toml`). There is no contributor license
agreement and no copyright assignment, and you keep your copyright. This is
deliberate: it means the project cannot be relicensed out from under you.

## What helps most

- Connectors for devices and services.
- Compatible devices and their firmware.
- Trying the kitchen device and the e-ink board on your own hardware and reporting
  what breaks.
- Documentation and translations.

## Before you start

Open an issue for anything large, so we agree on the shape first. Read
`docs/decisions.md`: a change that undoes a decision needs a new decision record,
not a quiet edit.

## Privacy

Never commit personal data, recordings, body photos or secrets.
`docs/privacy-and-recording.md` is the rule, and it comes before any feature.
